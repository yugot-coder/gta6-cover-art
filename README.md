# Neon Coast — GTA-style Cover Maker

Turn any photo and caption into a GTA 6-style multi-panel cover, offline in your browser. Five layouts (hero + 3, nine-panel grid, full bleed, split four, tilted snapshot), five sunset/neon colour grades, bold Anton lettering with a Pacifico script subtitle, per-panel crop and positioning, undo/redo — and one-step PNG export at 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350 and 1920 × 1080.

Serve `app/` with any static server (`node serve.ts`, then open http://localhost:8080) and make your cover: add photos, pick a layout and grade, write the title, export. Your photos never leave your machine. No Rockstar logo, artwork or font is used — the fonts are Anton and Pacifico, shipped under the SIL Open Font License in `app/fonts/`.
